## Daily structure

1. **Review — 10 minutes:** delayed retrieval of yesterday's corrections plus older items due for review.
2. **Input — 15–20 minutes:** Duolingo, general Listening, or Reading.
3. **Speaking — 20–25 minutes:** answers, role-play, shadowing, or a timed recording.
4. **Writing/Reading — 15–20 minutes:** a practical message, paragraph, or comprehension task.
5. **Correction — 10 minutes:** reproduce corrected sentences without copying.
6. **Measurement — 5 minutes:** record accuracy, response length, recurring errors, and mastered items.

Record each session's start, end, estimated active duration, result, and next review date in `PRACTICE_LOG.md`. Use these records to revise forecasts only after enough evidence has accumulated.

Use spaced review after **1 day, 3 days, 7 days, 14 days, and 30 days**. Sunday is normally a lighter review and measurement day.

## First 30 days

### Week 1 — Baseline and everyday foundations

#### Day 1 — 2026-08-11: Speaking baseline
- Learn routine and frequency language: `usually`, `often`, `sometimes`, `rarely`.
- Give six quick answers and one 45–60 second answer about a normal day.
- Review `feel tired`, `stay up late`, and the present simple for routines.
- Measure response time, answer length, pauses, clarity, and grammar errors.

#### Day 2 — 2026-08-12: Listening baseline
- Practise predicting the topic and listening for key facts.
- Shadow five short sentences and summarize the audio in 3–5 sentences.
- Review Day 1 corrections.
- Measure main-idea and detail accuracy.

#### Day 3 — 2026-08-13: Reading baseline
- Practise skimming, scanning, and identifying unsupported claims.
- Read a short general-interest text and answer factual and inference questions.
- Review hotel questions and direct/indirect question order.
- Measure accuracy and completion time.

#### Day 4 — 2026-08-14: Writing baseline
- Learn a simple paragraph structure: main idea, details, closing sentence.
- Write 100–120 words about work, study, or a normal weekday.
- Review Day 2 Listening language.
- Measure meaning, grammar, vocabulary, cohesion, and independence.

#### Day 5 — 2026-08-15: Restaurants and food
- Learn ordering, recommendations, preferences, allergies, and prices.
- Complete a restaurant role-play and speak for 45 seconds about food preferences.
- Review `non-spicy food`, `a cup of coffee`, and `Could you recommend ...?`.
- Measure successful requests and article/plural errors.

#### Day 6 — 2026-08-16: Directions and transportation
- Learn `turn left/right`, `go straight`, `get on/off`, and `How long does it take?`.
- Ask for directions and explain a route to work.

## Mastery and adaptation rules

- **Advance:** at least 80% accuracy twice on different days without a model.
- **Review:** 70–79% accuracy or one important corrected error returns.
- **Repeat or simplify:** below 70%, unclear meaning, or several basic errors block communication.
- **Increase difficulty:** answers are accurate, natural, and comfortable within the time limit.
- **Do not overtrain one task:** use spaced review while continuing Speaking and new topics.
- Update `LEARNER_PROFILE.md` after each measured session and revise this plan every seven days when evidence supports a change.
