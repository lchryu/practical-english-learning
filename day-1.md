# DAY 1 — READ, COPY, AND IMITATE

## Topic

**Introducing Yourself**

## Goals

- Understand the structure of a self-introduction.
- Become familiar with the key sentence patterns.
- Speak for 30–60 seconds using the model essay as a guide.

## Total Time

**75–90 minutes**

## 1. Read the Model Essay

> Hi, I’m Alex. At the moment, I spend most of my time learning programming, improving my English, and working on personal projects. I have already graduated from university, so I am not completely new to technology. However, there are still many practical skills that I want to develop.
>
> I would describe myself as curious and ambitious. When I become interested in something, I usually want to understand it deeply. One of my strengths is that I can learn new concepts quite quickly. However, I sometimes try to learn too many things at once, which makes it difficult for me to stay focused.
>
> My current goal is to become more confident in English. I can understand quite a lot when I listen or read, but speaking and writing are still challenging for me. Over the next six months, I want to practise every day until English becomes a natural part of my life.

## 2. Read Aloud

Read the model essay in three rounds.

### Round 1 — Slow Reading

Read slowly and pronounce every word clearly. Do not worry about sounding like a native speaker.

### Round 2 — Chunk Reading

Read in meaningful groups instead of reading one word at a time.

```text
At the moment /
I spend most of my time /
learning programming /
improving my English /
and working on personal projects.
```

### Round 3 — Natural Reading

Read the entire essay more naturally and record yourself. Afterward, check:

- Are there any words that you cannot pronounce?
- Are there any parts where you often hesitate?
- Are there any sentences that feel too long?

## 3. Understand the Structure

### Paragraph 1 — Current Situation

- Who you are
- What you currently do
- Your previous experience
- What you still want to improve

### Paragraph 2 — Personality

- Your personality
- One of your strengths
- One of your weaknesses
- How your personality affects your life

### Paragraph 3 — Goals

- Your current goal
- Your current difficulty
- Your six-month target

## 4. Key Sentence Patterns

```text
At the moment, I am...
Currently, I spend most of my time...
I have already...
I have some experience with...
I would describe myself as...
One of my strengths is...
One thing I struggle with is...
When I become interested in something, I...
My current goal is to...
The main reason I want to... is that...
Over the next six months, I want to...
I hope to become...
```

## 5. Copy and Personalize the Essay

Copy the model essay once. Then replace the following details with your own information:

| Model information | Your information |
| --- | --- |
| Alex | Your name or English name |
| learning programming | Your current activity |
| improving my English | Another regular activity |
| personal projects | Your work or projects |
| curious and ambitious | Two adjectives that describe you |
| learn new concepts quickly | One of your strengths |
| learn too many things | One of your weaknesses |
| confident in English | Your current goal |

## 7. Speak for 30–60 Seconds

Look only at these keywords and introduce yourself. Do not read the full essay.

```text
name
current activities
education
personality
strength
weakness
English goal
```

If you need time to think, use one of these expressions:

```text
Let me think for a moment.
What I want to say is...
Another thing about me is...
I’m not sure how to explain this, but...
```

## End-of-Day Checklist

- [ ] I copied the model essay once.
- [ ] I completed the personal introduction with my own information.
- [ ] I recorded myself speaking for 30–60 seconds.
- [ ] I made a list of five words or phrases that were difficult to say.

> Your goal today is not to speak perfectly. Your goal is to understand the structure and begin speaking about yourself.
