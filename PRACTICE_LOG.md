## Future-session rule

At the first substantive exercise, record the session start time. At a break or session end, record the end time and estimate active practice duration, excluding long idle gaps when identifiable. Record:

- Skill and topic.
- Exercise count or word count.
- Accuracy or score.
- Important recurring errors.
- Whether the evidence was verified, provisional, or self-reported.
- The next planned review date.

## Forecasting rule

- Do not update target dates from a single strong or weak task.
- Recalculate forecasts after at least seven logged practice days or at each four-week checkpoint.
- Use active hours, practice frequency, delayed-retention results, and four-skill scores.
- Report a target window rather than a guaranteed date.
