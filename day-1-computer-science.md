# Day 1 — Introducing Yourself Through Computer Science

## Goals

By the end of this 75–90 minute lesson, you will be able to:

- organize a personal introduction into three paragraphs;
- describe your computer science studies, interests, and experience;
- discuss your personality, a strength, and a challenge;
- explain how computer science supports your English learning; and
- speak about yourself for 30–60 seconds using keywords.

## 2. Model introduction

Read once without stopping. Try to understand the main ideas from context.

> Hi, I’m Minh. At the moment, I spend most of my time studying computer science, practising programming, and improving my English. I am especially interested in software development because I enjoy using technology to solve real-world problems. I already have some experience with Python and Java, but there are still many technical and practical skills that I want to develop.
>
> I would describe myself as curious and determined. When I become interested in a technical topic, I usually explore it deeply and try to create a small project with it. One of my strengths is that I can work patiently on difficult problems. However, I sometimes try to learn too many things at once, which makes it difficult for me to stay focused and finish projects.
>
> My current goal is to improve my English through computer science. I read programming documentation, watch technical videos, and learn new vocabulary while I code. I can understand many technical explanations, but speaking and writing are still challenging for me. Over the next six months, I want to discuss technical ideas, ask clear questions, and communicate with other developers more confidently.

## 3. Structure

### Paragraph 1 — Current situation

- Who you are
- What you are studying
- Your interests and experience
- Skills you want to develop

### Paragraph 2 — Personality

- Two adjectives that describe you
- One strength and one challenge
- How the challenge affects you

### Paragraph 3 — English goal

- How you practise English through computer science
- What you can already do
- What is still difficult
- Your six-month target

## 4. Key vocabulary

| Phrase | Meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| software development | creating software | I am interested in software development. |
| documentation | written information explaining software | I read English documentation. |
| technical topic | a subject related to technology | This video explains a technical topic. |
| real-world problem | a problem outside an exercise | I want to solve real-world problems. |
| practical skill | an ability used in real situations | Projects develop practical skills. |
| debug | find and correct errors in code | I am debugging my program. |
| curious | interested in understanding things | I am curious about computers. |
| determined | unwilling to give up | I am determined to improve. |
| challenging | difficult but useful | Speaking is challenging for me. |

## 5. Key sentence patterns

### Current situation

```text
At the moment, I am studying...
Currently, I spend most of my time + V-ing...
Right now, I am focusing on...
I am especially interested in...
```

> At the moment, I am studying computer science.
>
> Currently, I spend most of my time learning Python and practising English.
>
> Right now, I am focusing on backend development.

### Experience

```text
I have already...
I have some experience with...
I am not completely new to...
However, I still want to improve...
```

> I have already completed a few small projects.
>
> I have some experience with Python, Git, and databases.
>
> However, I still want to improve my problem-solving skills.

### Personality

```text
I would describe myself as...
When I become interested in something, I usually...
One of my strengths is...
One thing I struggle with is + V-ing...
```

> I would describe myself as curious and patient.
>
> One of my strengths is that I do not give up easily.
>
> One thing I struggle with is staying focused on one project.

### English goals

```text
My current goal is to...
I improve my English by + V-ing...
I can already..., but...is still difficult for me.
The main reason I want to...is that...
Over the next six months, I want to...
```

> I improve my English by reading documentation and watching programming videos.
>
> I can already read simple documentation, but explaining my code is still difficult for me.
>
> Over the next six months, I want to communicate with developers more confidently.

### Grammar reminders

Use **V-ing** after `spend time` and `by`:

```text
Correct: I spend time learning Python.
Incorrect: I spend time to learn Python.

Correct: I learn by building projects.
Incorrect: I learn by build projects.
```

Use **to + base verb** after `want`:

```text
Correct: I want to improve my English.
Incorrect: I want improve my English.
```

## 6. Read aloud

### Round 1 — Slow reading

Read the model slowly. Pronounce every word clearly. Clarity is more important than speed or accent.


- Did I pronounce the final sounds in **spend**, **skills**, and **projects**?
- Did I pause between ideas rather than individual words?
- Which words were difficult?
- Where did I hesitate?
- Which sentence felt too long?

## 9. Speaking practice

### Step 1 — Read

Read your complete introduction aloud once.

### Step 2 — Make keyword notes

```text
name:
current study:
CS interest:
experience:
personality:
strength:
challenge:
English practice:
six-month goal:
```

### Step 3 — Speak for 30–60 seconds

Look only at the keywords. Do not repeat your written text word for word. Record yourself and continue even if you make a mistake.

Use these expressions when you need thinking time:

```text
Let me think for a moment.
What I want to say is...
Another thing about me is...
For example,...
I’m not sure how to explain this, but...
What I mean is...
```
